# Chores: routines, taking turns and "when needed"

## 🔢 Routine order

On Chores & Tasks (👥 By person), the **🔢** button on someone's column puts their chores and
to-dos in the order they should do them. Use ↑ ↓, then **✓ Done**. A parent can set anyone's
order, and each person can set their own.

- **Numbers:** they show on the cards (1, 2, 3…) on Chores & Tasks, the Family Board and the floor
  plan's person popup.
- **Every job is listed:** the order covers all of the person's jobs, including weekly ones that
  aren't on today's list, so they keep their place. One-time jobs that are already done are left
  out, and a one-time job checked off today (still shown crossed out) loses its number, so the
  rest stay numbered 1, 2, 3…
- **Unordered jobs:** jobs the person hasn't put in order come after the ordered ones.

**On the floor plan**, the avatar stands at the first unfinished job in that order. When it's
checked off, the avatar walks to the next one along the same curved line the 👣 path draws. If
the next job is in the same spot, the avatar spins around and bounces a couple of times instead.


## 🔄 Taking turns (parents only)

Use this for a chore that the kids rotate through, like emptying the dishwasher.

1. Open the chore's ✎ form and pick a repeating schedule.
2. Tick **🔄 Take turns — each time it's done, it goes to the next person**.
3. Add the people and put them in order with ↑ ↓.
4. Mark who's **Up now**.

**How turns pass.** The chore sits on one person until it's done, then it goes to the next
person, and after the last person back to the first. Turns follow the chore being done, not the
calendar. If Sam doesn't do it today, it's still Sam's tomorrow. A day it was done counts as one
turn, even when it has morning and evening slots.

**Where it shows.** Each day the chore is in that person's column, progress bars, floor-plan
walk, history and Prize Bank rewards. When it's done, it shows as done by whoever did it. The
card's **🔄 next: …** badge says whose turn is after that.

**Who can change it.** Only a parent can set up, change or stop a rotation. Once parents have
passwords, the server enforces this as well. A rotating chore can't be dragged to another
person's column; change it in ✎ instead.

## 🔔 When needed

Choose the **When needed** schedule for a chore that doesn't come up on a fixed schedule, like
the dishwasher, the trash or the litter box.

- **While it's waiting**, it's on nobody's list. It sits in the **When needed** strip at the top of
  Chores & Tasks, showing whose turn it is if it rotates.
- **When it needs doing**, anyone taps **🔔** on it. It then shows on that person's list until
  someone checks it off.
- **Once it's done**, it goes back to waiting for the next 🔔. Unchecking it puts it back on the
  list.
- **Combined with 🔄 Take turns**, each 🔔 lands on the next person in the rotation.

This doesn't count toward "missed chores" in the stats, since nothing was due.

Limit: a "when needed" chore can be done once per day. A 🔔 on the same day it was finished
shows it the next day.

## ⏱ How much: times and hours

In the add/edit form, **How much** can be:

- **Just check it off.** This is the default, and how every job worked before.
- **A number of times**, for example basketball practice 5 times at 30 minutes each. Each ＋ counts one time of that many minutes (the minutes are optional; they show on the card and in ✅ To approve). Tap **＋** each time it's done. The job is checked off when all the times are done.
- **A number of hours**, for example 6 hours of weeding, in half hours. Tap **＋**, then pick how much was done: 30 minutes, 1 hour and so on, or all the rest. 30 minutes is the smallest chunk.

The card shows a progress bar such as "⏱ 2½ of 6 h". **↶** takes back the last chunk. Tapping the ✓ on a finished job takes back its last chunk, and the job is no longer done.

A one-time job can take as many days as it needs. A repeating job starts from zero each time it comes up. Any reward on the job is paid once, when the goal is reached.

## ✅ Needs a parent's OK

Parents can tick **✅ Needs a parent's OK** on a chore or to-do, or on a prize.

- **When it's ticked:** in the chore form it's at the top. It's ticked automatically when you turn on a Prize Bank reward for a new to-do. New prizes have it ticked by default. Untick it any time.
- **When a kid logs a chunk of a times or hours job** (30 minutes of weeding, one practice): each chunk waits in ✅ To approve on its own, and the card shows **⏳ 30 min waiting for OK**. It counts once a parent says yes; when the approved chunks reach the goal, the job is checked off. ✗ No takes just that chunk off.
- **When a kid checks off such a job:** the card shows **⏳ Waiting for a parent's OK**. It doesn't count as done, and doesn't pay its reward, until a parent says yes. The kid can tap ⏳ to take it back.
- **When a kid claims such a prize:** the claim waits for a parent. Any stars it costs are held until then.
- **When a parent checks it off:** it counts straight away.

**✅ To approve** appears in the menu for parents, with a red count when something is waiting. It lists each kid's waiting items with **✓ Yes** / **✗ No**, plus **✓ Yes to all** per kid.

- **Yes:** the job counts as done and pays its reward, or the prize pays out.
- **No:** the job goes back to not done. For a times or hours job, only the last chunk comes off. A refused prize claim gives back any stars it cost.

Jobs and prizes you already had don't need an OK unless you tick it.

## ⧉ Copy to someone else (parents)

Open a chore or to-do (✎), tap **⧉ Copy to…**, tick one or more people, then tap **⧉ Copy**.

- Each person gets their own copy, with the same title, notes, icon, schedule, time of day, room and spot, how much, and reward.
- Each person checks off their own copy, so one kid finishing doesn't finish it for the other.
- Taking turns isn't copied; each copy belongs to just that person.
- The copy is made from the job as saved, so save any changes first.

## 🎬 Rewards that aren't money or stars

A Prize Bank to-do's reward can be **Stars**, **Money** or **Something else**. Choose **Something else** and type what the kid earns, for example "Movie time", "Friend time" or "Special trip".

Set it in the chore/to-do form (parents), on chores as well as to-dos, or under **Settings → Prize Bank — extra to-do rewards**.

**Emoji:** every reward can have one. Tap the emoji next to the reward type to change it. Without one, money shows 💲, stars ⭐ and something else 🎁. The emoji shows on the job's card next to the reward.

When the kid finishes the job, the reward goes on their **🎉 Earned to enjoy** list in their Prize Bank column.

- A parent taps **✓ Used** once the reward has been enjoyed, and it moves to **Already enjoyed** on the kid's 📜 Past prizes page.
- **Not yet** there moves it back.
- Unchecking the job takes back the reward, as long as it hasn't been used yet.

## 🎁 Prize Bank page

The Prize Bank page shows only prize boxes. Pick **👥 Board** or **🏠 Map**:

- **👥 Board:** one column per kid with their ⭐ stars and money, links to their 🏦 Bank and 📜 Past prizes, and their **🎁 Prize boxes** with a progress bar for each job. Under each box are today's jobs for it, to check off (＋ a chunk) right there.
- **🏠 Map:** the floor plan with just the prize-box jobs, so kids can walk through them like the regular map.

Rewards from to-dos ("Movie time") wait under **🎉 Earned to enjoy** on the kid's 📜 Past prizes page until a parent taps ✓ Used.

**Elsewhere:** prize-box jobs are left off Chores & Tasks and the Family Board, both 👥 by person and the floor plan; they're on the Prize Bank board and map. When a kid is picked, either map shows only their own avatar and jobs (plus ones anyone can claim).

## 🎁 Prize boxes

**Adding one.** On the Prize Bank page, a parent taps **+ Add a prize box for …** in a kid's column, or **✎ Edit · add jobs** on a box. Prize boxes can also be set up under **Settings → Prize Bank — prizes**.

- **For:** tick one or more kids. With nobody ticked, it's for everyone. Each kid only sees their own boxes and everyone's boxes, and earns them on their own.
- **What they get:**
  - **Money:** paid into their first bank account when it's redeemed. With no bank account it goes to their earned Prize Bank money instead.
  - **Stars:** added to their star count.
  - **Something else:** type it in, for example "Movie night" or "Beach trip". It's just marked redeemed.
- **Emoji:** each prize can have one. Without one, a money prize shows 💲, a stars prize ⭐ and anything else 🎁.
- **How it's earned:**
  - **Spending stars.**
  - **Doing chores / to-dos:** one or more jobs, each with how much of it is needed, for example 4 hours of weeding + 4 hours of mulching. An hours job counts the hours put in; any other job counts the times it was done. Pick jobs with the box's job list, add **+ Add another job**, or make a brand-new one with **+ New chore / to-do** (it's added to the box when saved).
- **🔁 Can be earned again:** off for new boxes, so once redeemed a box moves to the kid's 📜 Past prizes. Turn it on for something they can earn over and over. Prizes made before this change can all be earned again.

**From a chore or to-do.** A parent can open a job's ✎ form and use **🎁 Counts toward a prize** to add it to one of that kid's prize boxes, or to start a new one. **Take off** removes it again.

**✓ Redeemed (parents).** Each box has a **Redeemed** tick box.

- **Ticking it** pays the prize out right away (money to the bank, stars to the count) and moves a one-time box to Past prizes. A parent can do this even if the jobs aren't all finished. If the kid had claimed it and it's waiting for an OK, ticking it approves it.
- **Unticking it** on the 📜 Past prizes page takes back what it paid (removes the bank deposit, or the stars) and puts the box back on the board. For a box that can be earned again, **↶ undo last** on the board does the same.

**Kids** can tap **Redeem** once a box is earned. If the box needs a parent's OK, the claim waits in ✅ To approve.

**📜 Past prizes** lists a kid's redeemed boxes, newest first, with what each paid and when, plus the rewards they've already enjoyed.

## Data

Only new things were added:

- **Columns on `tasks`:** `rotation`, `rotation_start`, `needed_since`, and `goal_type`, `goal_amount`, `goal_minutes`.
- **Columns on `prizes`:** `reward_kind`, `reward_amount`, `emoji`.
- **More columns on `tasks`:** `reward_text`, `reward_emoji`.
- **New tables:**
  - `task_positions`: each person's routine order.
  - `task_progress`: the chunks of a times or hours job.
  - `prize_requirements`: the jobs a prize needs.
  - `earned_rewards`: custom rewards a kid has earned, and when each was used.
  - `pending_completions`: kids' check-offs waiting for a parent's OK.
- **Waiting chunks:** `task_progress.pending` (1 while a kid's chunk waits for an OK).
- **Approval columns:** `tasks.needs_approval`, `prizes.needs_approval`, `prize_redemptions.status` (`'pending'` while waiting).
- **Prize boxes:** the `prize_members` table (who a prize is for), `prizes.once`, and `prize_redemptions.bank_transaction_id`, `paid_money`, `paid_stars` (what redeeming paid, so it can be undone).

No existing data changes.
